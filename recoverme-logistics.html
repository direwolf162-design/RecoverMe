FAQ

Good questions.

Can't see yours? Call or email — we're happy to help.

How quickly can you reach me?

For emergency call-outs we aim to arrive within about 45 minutes, depending on location and traffic. We'll give you a realistic ETA when we confirm.

Are vehicles and furniture insured in transit?

Yes. Every vehicle and item we move is covered by our goods-in-transit and recovery insurance from collection to drop-off.

Can you move cars that don't run?

Absolutely — non-runners, seized wheels and accident-damaged vehicles. Just choose the right condition when booking so we bring the correct equipment.

Do you work nights and weekends?

We operate 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, including bank holidays. An out-of-hours supplement applies between 10pm and 6am.

When do I pay?

Your choice: a card deposit to secure the booking, bank transfer in advance, or payment to the driver when the job's complete.
